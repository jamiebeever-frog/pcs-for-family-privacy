Privacy Policy

PCS for Family

Last updated: October 1, 2026

PCS for Family is a calm planning and orientation tool for military families. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and what choices you have.

Who provides the app

PCS for Family is provided by Jamie Beever, an individual developer. It is independent and is not an official Department of Defense, military service, or government product.

What information the app handles

You may choose to enter household profile and move information, such as a destination, move dates, planning details, and children’s first names or ages. This information is stored locally on your device for the app’s planning features. PCS for Family does not require an account or login.

In version 1, we do not currently send this household information to a developer-operated server. Do not enter information you do not want stored on the device.

Advertising and analytics

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in version 1. We do not sell personal data. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is used in a way that affects your information.

Links to other sites

The app may link to official DoD, military, and government websites so you can verify current information. Those links take you out of PCS for Family. The linked sites have their own privacy policies and data practices, which we do not control.

Children’s privacy

PCS for Family is intended for adults planning a family move. It is not directed to children under 13 as users. A parent or other adult may enter a child’s first name or age for PCS planning, and that information remains local to the device in version 1. We do not knowingly collect children’s information from children or maintain a developer-operated account for them.

How to delete local information

To remove the app’s locally stored information, uninstall PCS for Family from your device. Device and operating-system behavior can vary, so review your device’s uninstall and storage controls if needed. If you keep a backup of the device, that backup may retain app data under your device provider’s policies.

Your privacy choices and rights

Because the app’s planning information is stored locally and no mandatory account is used, you can generally access or remove it through the app and your device. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have rights to know about, access, correct, delete, or limit certain uses of personal information, and to receive equal service when exercising those rights. We do not sell personal data. Contact us with a privacy request and we will explain what we can do for the information we control.
